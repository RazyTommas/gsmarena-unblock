## Samsung Baseband Security Gap

Analyzed 82 Samsung devices from FOTA data. 18 have separate modem (CP) firmware. Of those, 8 currently show modem lag:

**Flagship modem lag (latest firmware):**
- Galaxy S24 / S24+ / S24 Ultra: AP=Aug 2026, Modem=May 2026 → **3-month lag**
- Galaxy S21 / S21+ / S21 Ultra: AP=Mar 2026, Modem=Jan 2026 → **2-month lag**
- Galaxy S21 FE: AP=Jun 2026, Modem=May 2026 → 1-month lag

**Historical patterns:**
- Average lag across all updates: 0.5-1.0 months
- Max historical lag seen: 9 months (A-series)
- Most A-series devices are currently sync (lag=0)

**Why this matters:** The ASPL (pda_month, 100% day-01 confirmed) tells you the AP firmware patch level but says nothing about the baseband. A Galaxy S24 Ultra showing '2026-08-01' ASPL actually has modem firmware from May 2026. The Qualcomm/Samsung Exynos modem has its own attack surface (baseband RCE CVEs appear every few months), and the 3-month modem lag means those CVEs remain exploitable for months after the AP firmware claims to be patched.

**Data:** samsung-modem-lag.csv has per-model AP date, modem date, lag, and average historical lag.
