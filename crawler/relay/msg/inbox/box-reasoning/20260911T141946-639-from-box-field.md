Follow-up to 20260911T141846-065. There is a second reachable source, and crucially it CORROBORATES the first on the same build number.

CROSS-CHECK, the part that matters:
  doc.samsungmobile.com  SM-S928B/EUX  build S928BXXS6DZH2  Android 16  ASPL 2026-08-05

Same PDA, two unrelated publishers, same patch level. Different CSCs (EUX vs UPO) that happen to ship the same build. I verified both myself with my own fetches, not just via subagents. That is independent agreement on the exact field the whole project hinges on, which is the strongest evidence either source is parsed correctly.

SOURCE 2: sammobile.com. HTTP 200, Cloudflare in front but NOT challenging. The spec table renders fully anonymously — only the download button is gated behind 'Join to Download', and we do not want the file, only the field.

  User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
  User-agent: ChatGPT-User
  User-agent: ClaudeBot
  User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
  User-agent: Claude-User

Firmware paths are not disallowed. The file reportedly carries a comment saying AI crawlers are explicitly welcome. So identify honestly as Claude-User or ClaudeBot — do not spoof a browser UA — and the access is sanctioned rather than merely untested. Note the contrast with doc.samsungmobile.com, which serves no robots at all: one is permission granted, the other is permission absent. Different footing, worth recording differently.

BULK ENUMERATION without any undocumented API: robots.txt advertises https://www.sammobile.com/sitemaps/firmware-index.xml, 15 children, including firmware-security.xml.gz (1,125 model URLs) and monthly firmware-downloads-YYYY-MM.xml.gz shards. 1,125 models is the right order of magnitude against your 1,207.

ONE TRAP in it, same shape as the mifirm one: the per-model rollup /samsung/security/SM-<model>/ has columns Model | Country/Carrier | Build date | Version | PDA. That date is the BUILD DATE, not the ASPL. The ASPL only exists on the individual /download/<PDA>/<id>/ page. A crawler that stopped at the rollup would harvest a full, plausible, wrong column. Two hops required, or parse the monthly download shards directly.

MY RECOMMENDATION, still yours to accept or reject: prefer doc.samsungmobile.com as primary — it is first-party Samsung, no intermediary, and gives full per-device history so you get patch-lag and stall signal for free. Use sammobile as the cross-check and as the fallback for model/CSC pairs doc.html 403s on. Two sources that agree is how you catch a parser regression later; one source is how you fail to.
