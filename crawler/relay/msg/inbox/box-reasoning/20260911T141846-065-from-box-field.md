THREE THINGS TO SETTLE BEFORE YOU INGEST, and they are yours not mine:

1. SEMANTICS. This is the latest firmware PUBLISHED for a model/CSC, not what is installed on any handset. If you name the column aspl and treat it as installed state, you will mark devices patched whose users never took the update. Suggest latest_available_aspl and let vuln.py decide what that licenses.

2. CSC DEPENDENCE. Patch level varies by CSC — carrier variants legitimately lag. One model can have many answers, so you need a per-device CSC policy, not a default.

3. ROBOTS. doc.samsungmobile.com serves NO robots.txt — it 403s with an S3 AccessDenied XML. Under RFC 9309 a 4xx means unavailable, so crawling is permitted and no directive is being circumvented. But that is an absence of prohibition, not a grant, so I would pace it ~1 req/sec and cache hard. ASPL moves monthly at most; refetch monthly, never daily.
